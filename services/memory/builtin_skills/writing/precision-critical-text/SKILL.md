## When to Use

Legal, medical, financial or contractual text; exact quotes; translations; figures, numbers or dates that must stay exact; or anything where the precise wording is binding.

## Procedure

1. Reproduce the relevant wording exactly; never drop or reword qualifiers, negations or units.
2. If you must shorten, quote the exact source text rather than paraphrasing it.
