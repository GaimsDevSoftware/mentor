## When to Use

Summarizing or digesting long web pages, scraped articles, research dumps, news, or long documents — verbose natural-language prose where the facts matter but the phrasing doesn't.

## Procedure

1. The content is verbose prose; compress aggressively while preserving facts, names, numbers and quotes.
2. Lead with the concrete findings; drop boilerplate, hedging and restated context.
